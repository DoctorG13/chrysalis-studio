# 🦋 Chrysalis

> The Business Operating System for Dressmakers

---

## Vision

Chrysalis exists to help independent dressmakers run their entire business without relying on paper diaries, notebooks or spreadsheets.

Our goal is simple:

> Help dressmakers spend less time managing their business and more time creating beautiful garments.

# Product Philosophy

Every feature must answer one question:

> Does this make Donna's day easier?

If not...

It waits.

---

# Design Principles

- Fast
- Simple
- Beautiful
- Reliable
- Professional

No unnecessary complexity.

---

# Success Criteria

Version 1.0 is complete when:

- Donna no longer needs her paper diary.
- Donna can confidently manage clients.
- Donna can manage fittings.
- Donna can manage jobs.
- Donna can manage payments.
- Donna can find everything quickly.
- Donna enjoys opening Chrysalis every morning.
