# Project History

A working history of the assistant and business-platform projects that led to BizziBuddi.

This document records milestones that can currently be supported by retained conversation history and repository records. It deliberately distinguishes confirmed facts from names and dates that still need evidence. It should be extended as earlier project records are recovered.

## At a glance

The project developed from a personal Telegram assistant called **Giles** into a broader business-management platform. The current product is **BizziBuddi**, with the original **Chrysalis Studio** application and database lineage still visible in parts of the repository.

The user has also recalled an earlier name, **Trive**, and possibly work before Giles. That earlier stage is not yet independently dated or documented here.

## Timeline

### 5 May 2026 — Giles is in active development

The earliest dated record currently confirmed in retained conversation history is 5 May 2026.

At that point, the project was a Telegram-based AI/business assistant running on Windows. The process had been called `my-bot` and was renamed or recreated as **Giles**.

The assistant was already being used for practical calendar tasks, including:

- checking what was on and what was coming next
- scheduling and cancelling events
- reminders and recurring events
- storing calendar data in SQLite
- working with Google Calendar

This is the earliest confirmed milestone found so far, not necessarily the project's actual beginning.

### 6 May 2026 — Giles ELITE expansion

A follow-up discussion asked to upgrade Giles. The recorded work included recurring events and reminders, with broader business-dashboard and AI-assistant capabilities discussed as future expansion.

A web dashboard for business use was also proposed. This marks an early move from a focused Telegram calendar assistant toward a more capable business assistant.

### Later evolution — from assistant to business platform

Subsequent project work developed the business-management direction that became **BizziBuddi**. The current repository describes BizziBuddi as a business operating system for independent makers, bringing People, Jobs, Calendar, Finance, Automation, Production, Reports and Buddi into one account-backed workspace.

The repository retains the name **Chrysalis Studio** in its project and server lineage. BizziBuddi became the product-facing name; Chrysalis Studio remains part of the underlying application history.

The exact dates and intermediate names for this transition have not yet been established from the currently retrieved historical records.

### 19 September 2026 — BizziBuddi development is explicitly underway

A retained conversation is titled **“Resume BizziBuddi Development”**, dated 19 September 2026. This is a confirmed later marker for the BizziBuddi project, but it is not the beginning of the overall project: Giles is already documented in May.

### October 2026 — production-readiness and real-business workflows

By October, work had expanded into a production-oriented business application, including:

- account and workspace isolation
- persistent People, Jobs, Calendar, Finance and Production records
- onboarding and a guided first-day experience
- invoice and payment workflows
- payment activity and audit history
- production deployment checks, database integrity checks and backup/restore preparation

The repository's release and workflow checks were used to validate deployment readiness.

### 15 November 2026 — Donna's Chrysalis Clothes workspace reaches production

A migration was completed for **Donna Hourigan / Chrysalis Clothes** on the live BizziBuddi Railway deployment.

The migration package contained 96 records across 12 tables. The production import verified the matching records were already present, confirmed the expected record counts, reported SQLite integrity as `ok`, and reported zero foreign-key violations. A verified pre-import backup was created.

Donna then successfully signed in to her live workspace. This was an important first real-business production milestone.
